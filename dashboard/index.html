    Method & evidence

    Seven-day growth: tested, not used

    We tested seven-day growth in both models. The growth experiment worsened leave-one-out error for the value model, and the odds variant with growth had worse walk-forward Brier than the shipped age-only fit. The shipped score omits growth.

    Sensitivity (independent re-computation)

    • #1 Polymarket International holds #1 in 64% of 1,000 bootstrap resamples of the 128 tokenized peers and stays in the top 5 in 99%; when #1 moves, it goes almost always to Steakhouse Financial.
    • The Prediction Market category offset (x2.54) rests on a single peer, Rain. Removing that offset moves Polymarket from #1 to #3 (confirmed by independent re-computation).
    • Neutralising every category offset based on one peer or fewer (12 categories) still leaves Polymarket #2; extending that to two peers or fewer (20 categories) leaves it #3. The top group's membership does not change.
    • The token-odds age coefficient is positive in all 500 bootstrap resamples and its Brier score is stable around 0.200 (spread 0.004). Ranking uncertainty comes from the value model's coefficients and thin-category offsets, not from the odds.
